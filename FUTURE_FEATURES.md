# TaskMaster Pro - Future Features

## Planned Enhancements

### 🌍 Timezone Automatic Calculation
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Description:** Automatically detect and adjust task times based on user's timezone
- **Benefits:** 
  - Tasks will show correct times for users in different timezones
  - Reminders and notifications will trigger at the right local time
  - Better UX for users traveling or collaborating across timezones
- **Status:** Not Started
